A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Make yourself comfortable.

Sit facing a window or soft light, place your camera at eye level, and keep your whole face inside the oval.

  • One expression at a time. Match the example, then capture when you’re ready.
  • Three seconds to get ready. Keep the expression until you hear the capture sound. For the eyes-closed step, enable sound first.
  • You’re in control. Review every photo and retake it as often as you like.
